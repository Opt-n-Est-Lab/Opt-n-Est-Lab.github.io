Reservoir Computing

Reservoir computing (RC) architectures that predict time-varying uncertainties in complex dynamical systems. By exploiting the computational efficiency of RC, we aim to predict unknown behaviors in dynamic environments.

Project details →

Distributionally Robust Optimization

Decision-making under uncertainty where the probability distribution of the uncertain parameter is unknown. We develop algorithms that stay reliable and robust against distribution shifts in real-world applications.

Project details →

Human-Robot Interaction in VR

VR technology used to reimagine experiences for user-friendly urban mobility. Integrating autonomous devices into virtual environments creates immersive, personalized experiences that forecast the next generation of urban transportation.

Project details →

Connected Smart Cars

A multi-level adaptive control architecture: the proactive level leverages data over a cloud network to cope with unforeseen environmental uncertainties, while the reactive level uses machine learning and robust adaptive control to compensate for them.

Project details →

Safe Planning under Large Uncertainties

Inspired by the Miracle on the Hudson, a decision-making architecture that quickly makes a sequence of decisions for autonomous systems, integrating neuroscience, control theory, and machine learning toward safety guarantees under large uncertainties.

Project details →

Cyber-Physical Systems Security

Safety-critical CPS applications — power systems, critical infrastructure, transportation networks, industrial control — have renewed interest in CPS security. We study and develop attack-resilient estimation and detection algorithms.
