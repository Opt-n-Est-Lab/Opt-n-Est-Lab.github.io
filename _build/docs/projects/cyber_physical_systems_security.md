Recent developments in Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and their safety-critical applications &mdash; power systems, critical infrastructure, transportation networks, and industrial control systems &mdash; have led to renewed interest in CPS security. In this project, we study and develop attack-resilient estimation and detection algorithms for CPS.
