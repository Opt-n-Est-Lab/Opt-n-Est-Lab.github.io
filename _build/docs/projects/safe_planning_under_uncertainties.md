Inspired by the Miracle on the Hudson, we aim to design a decision-making architecture that can quickly make a sequence of decisions for autonomous systems. We integrate neuroscience, control theory, and machine learning &mdash; leveraging the advantages of each &mdash; toward a safety guarantee under large uncertainties.
